## A — «10 YEARS»
Create a YouTube thumbnail, 16:9, 1280x720. Highly detailed digital painting in a bold cartoon style: thick dark ink outlines, rich cel shading, dramatic cinematic lighting, saturated colors, crisp and sharp, readable at small size.

CHARACTER (left 40% of the frame, waist-up, large, close to the camera): use the character from the attached reference sheet exactly — a round plain white head with no nose and no ears, small black oval eyes with tiny white highlights, worried eyebrows slanted up toward the middle, a small open frightened mouth, a white adhesive bandage on his cheek, an olive-green bucket hat with a dark band and a glowing headlamp on the front, a red knitted scarf, a dark teal zip-up rain jacket, white mitten hands, an orange backpack with a rolled sleeping mat on top. He leans with both forearms on a rusty metal railing on the flat roof of a supermarket, looking slightly toward the viewer with a scared, exhausted expression. His headlamp is ON and throws a bright beam of white light out into the dark city. Behind him on the roof stands a huge unlit store sign with big dead letters reading "MARKET", partly hidden behind him.

LIGHT: a glowing oil lantern sits on the railing at the far left and throws strong warm orange light onto his face and the left side of his body. His right edge gets a cold green rim light from the sky.

BACKGROUND (right 60%): a huge dead city at night after a solar storm — a skyline of dark skyscrapers with not a single light on, except a few tiny candle-lit windows. Above the city, an enormous, very bright aurora: green and magenta curtains filling the sky, reflecting on the buildings. One building on the horizon is burning with an orange glow and a column of smoke. Below, the supermarket parking lot: abandoned cars, one with its door open, a dead street lamp, cracked asphalt with a faint green reflection of the aurora.

TEXT: top right corner, huge bold white text "10 YEARS" in a heavy condensed sans-serif font, slightly italic, thick black outline and dark drop shadow, slightly grunge texture. Spell it exactly "10 YEARS". No other text anywhere except "MARKET" on the sign.

COMPOSITION: strong contrast between the warm lantern-lit character on the left and the cold green-blue apocalyptic city on the right. Dark vignette in the corners. No watermark, no logo, no borders.

## B — «DAY 1 → 10 YEARS LATER»
Create a YouTube thumbnail, 16:9, 1280x720, split vertically into two equal halves with a thin white divider. Highly detailed digital painting in a bold cartoon style: thick dark ink outlines, rich cel shading, dramatic lighting, saturated colors, crisp and readable at small size.

The same character appears in both halves, waist-up, large, centered in each half. Use the character from the attached reference sheet exactly — a round plain white head with no nose and no ears, small black oval eyes, a white adhesive bandage on his cheek, an olive-green bucket hat with a dark band and a headlamp on the front, a red knitted scarf, a dark teal zip-up rain jacket, white mitten hands, an orange backpack with a rolled sleeping mat.

LEFT HALF — the first night: inside a bright supermarket aisle full of colorful products, the ceiling lights exploding with sparks, and through the big front windows a green and magenta aurora fills the night sky. The character looks shocked: eyes wide, mouth open, one blue sweat drop, clean new clothes, headlamp off. Top label: a black brush-stroke banner with white bold italic text "DAY 1", with the "1" in red.

RIGHT HALF — ten years later: the same supermarket abandoned and overgrown, empty broken shelves covered in vines and moss, a hole in the roof with the aurora visible through it, a lantern glowing warm on the floor. The character is ten years older: a thick brown beard with grey streaks, a faded torn bucket hat, a dirty patched teal jacket, a frayed red scarf, tired haunted eyes, his headlamp ON with a bright beam cutting through the darkness. Top label: a black brush-stroke banner with bold italic text "10 YEARS LATER", where "10 YEARS" is white and "LATER" is yellow.

A thick curved red arrow with a black outline goes from the left character to the right character across the divider, near the bottom. Spell the texts exactly: "DAY 1" and "10 YEARS LATER". No other text, no watermark, no logo.
