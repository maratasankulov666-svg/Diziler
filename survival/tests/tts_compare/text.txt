You fell asleep in the back row of a shopping mall cinema. When you wake up, the screen is dark, the doors are locked, and the whole mall is silent. Day one. No guards. No cleaners. Every store is still lit, but nobody is coming. So you do the math. A food court with twelve freezers. Hundreds of water bottles in the vending machines. Enough for one person. Maybe for a year. Night one. Somewhere below you, an escalator starts moving on its own.
