# Уникальный герой канала — промпты для Nano Banana (29.11)
Жёлтая шапка занята (One Year Inside), чёрная голова — Your Other Life. Нужен свой силуэт: простой для анимации кодом (круглая голова, тело-палочки), узнаваемый по 2–3 ярким деталям.

## Вариант 1 (рекомендую): «панама + налобный фонарь»
Create a character reference sheet for an original cartoon mascot of a survival-story YouTube channel. Plain light grey background, no text.
Show the same character 5 times in a row: front view, three-quarter view, side view, back view, and front view waving. Below, a row of 6 face close-ups: worried, scared, determined, tired, relieved, smiling.
The character: a simple cartoon man, full body. A perfectly round, plain white head with no nose and no ears, two small black oval eyes with tiny white highlights, simple expressive eyebrows, a simple line mouth. Thin black stick legs and stick arms with round white mitten hands, dark brown boots.
His unique look: an olive-green bucket hat (fisherman hat) with a wide floppy brim, and a headlamp strapped around the hat with a glowing lamp in front. A red knitted scarf wrapped around his neck with the ends hanging down. A dark teal rain jacket with a hood down, zipper and two big pockets. A small white adhesive bandage on his left cheek. A worn orange backpack with a rolled sleeping mat on top.
Style: bold cartoon, thick dark ink outlines, clean cel shading, flat saturated colors, consistent proportions in every view (the head is about one third of the total height). Crisp, high resolution.
